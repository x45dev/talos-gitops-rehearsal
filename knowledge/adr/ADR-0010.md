# ADR-0010 - Keep the spin-up success metric as a stated target but deliberately uninstrumented in v1

Closes the orphaned commitment left by the 2026-07-25 descope of feature 002
(`knowledge/specs/002-phase-4-lifecycle-hardening/`), recorded as its T015.

Provenance, stated plainly: the substance of this decision is the maintainer's, made when they
reported that spin-up time has not been painful in daily use and directed the descope.
The *form* of the fix - an ADR plus a correction to the stale claims, rather than a correction
alone - was the agent's judgment between the two options T015 recorded, taken on the maintainer's
instruction to proceed.
Either half is reversible if that reading was wrong.

## Context

`docs/planning/PRD.md` Section 7 sets the project's first success metric: spin-up under 10 minutes
from the `mise` command to every payload `HelmRelease`/`Kustomization` reporting `Ready`.
It goes further than stating the number, and commits to how the number is defended:

> The plan (Phase 4) carries a per-stage budget; if the measured total exceeds the target, the metric
> is renegotiated explicitly (scope or number), never silently missed.

Three documents depended on that sentence.
`docs/planning/PLAN-ephemeral-gitops-idp-2026-07-05.md` Phase 4 item 3 assigned the measurement and
the per-stage budget.
`knowledge/constitution.md`'s effective definition of done lists the metric, citing PRD Section 7.
Feature 002 was the vehicle that would have built the measurement.

The 2026-07-25 descope withdrew that workstream (former FR3/FR4, spec 002 v0.2.0): with spin-up
reported as not painful in real use, a measurement harness and committed budget would have defended a
metric that was not hurting anyone.
That decision stands. What it left behind is the problem this ADR closes: the commitment "never
silently missed" was carried by Phase 4, Phase 4 no longer carries it, and nothing replaced it.
An uninstrumented metric whose own PRD forbids silent misses is precisely the state that PRD sentence
was written to prevent, so leaving the documents as they were would have made the repository claim
something untrue about itself.

## Decision

1. **The under-ten-minute spin-up metric remains a stated target and is not renegotiated.**
   Its number and definition are unchanged. This ADR does not lower the bar.
2. **It is deliberately uninstrumented in v1.** No measurement harness, no per-stage budget, and no
   automated check exists or is required. Whether the target is currently met is, honestly, unknown.
3. **The documents that claimed a carrier are corrected**, so none of them asserts a budget that does
   not exist: PRD Section 7's carrier sentence, PLAN Phase 4 item 3, and the constitution's
   definition-of-done entry now point here instead.
4. **Revisit trigger.** Instrumentation is built when any of these holds: spin-up becomes noticeable
   in daily use; a payload component's readiness visibly dominates a run; or the cloud (CAPA)
   milestone arrives, where the timing carries over and a second person's expectations are involved.
   The withdrawn design is retained in the 002 plan, so a revisit is a lookup rather than a redesign.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Correct the stale sentences only, with no ADR.**
   Rejected: the correction would record *what* changed but not *why*, and the reasoning (a
   single-user tool, an unmeasured impression traded against harness cost, a named revisit trigger) is
   exactly the durable content this repository keeps in ADRs rather than in edits to working material.
   It would also leave no governed record, since the PRD and PLAN are ungoverned (ADR-0008).
2. **Renegotiate the metric explicitly, as PRD Section 7's own escape hatch allows.**
   Rejected: the escape hatch exists for a *measured* total that exceeds the target. Nothing has been
   measured, so there is no evidence on which to move the number, and changing it would substitute one
   unfounded claim for another. Leaving the target intact and admitting it is unverified is the honest
   position.
3. **Keep feature 002's measurement workstream after all.**
   Rejected by the descope this ADR follows: the maintainer's report is that the metric is not
   currently a problem. The cost of that call is real and is recorded as the "descope regret" risk in
   the 002 plan, not hidden here.
4. **Say nothing and let the documents stand.**
   Rejected: it is the state PRD Section 7 forbids, and it would leave three documents asserting a
   per-stage budget that no work carries.

## Consequences

- The repository no longer claims a measurement it does not perform. That is a weaker position than
  the original PRD sentence intended, and it is now visible rather than implied.
- A future spin-up regression is noticed by feel rather than caught by a number. This is the accepted
  cost; the revisit trigger is the mitigation.
- The constitution's definition of done keeps the metric, annotated as uninstrumented. A future
  promotion of the constitution past `active` should weigh whether a definition-of-done entry that
  nothing verifies is acceptable at `canonical`.
- Feature 002's surviving scope is unaffected: it still proves constitution invariants 3 (idempotency)
  and 4 (zero-residue teardown), which were never about timing.
