# ADR-0008 - Keep the PRD as an unmanaged extraction source, not a governed document

Records how this repository's PRD (`docs/planning/PRD.md`) is handled under RKA,
after a brief induction into the governed knowledge base was tried and reverted.
Accepted by the human maintainer on 2026-07-22: the maintainer directed that the
recommendation be followed and the decision noted, and the agent scribed this
record of it.

## Context

This repository was brought under RKA by brownfield discovery (2026-07-13), which
extracted the PRD's durable content into the governed constitution, context, and ADRs
and left the PRD document itself in place as working material.
The constitution recorded an open question: should `docs/planning/PRD.md` be inducted as
a governed document, or kept ungoverned?

On 2026-07-22 the PRD was briefly inducted into `knowledge/PRD.md` (`type: prd`).
Reviewing that against the RKA pattern surfaced the tension it creates:

- RKA ADR-0008 (the reference standard) makes a pre-existing, PRD-shaped document an
  extraction source, never an induction candidate.
  A PRD is a forward-looking document about intended change; brownfield discovery
  recovers backward-looking durable knowledge (decisions, invariants, patterns).
  They are different kinds of artifact, and discovery's contract deliberately omits
  specs and PRDs.
- This repository's durable knowledge was already extracted from the PRD into the
  now-`active` constitution and context and the ADRs.
  A second governed `knowledge/PRD.md` therefore reintroduces exactly the
  PRD/constitution redundancy RKA works to eliminate, with two documents to keep from
  drifting.
- RFC-001 section 2: a document outside the governed root carries no lifecycle `status`.
  The clean state for a non-inducted PRD is unmanaged working material with no `status`
  field.

## Decision

The PRD is kept as an unmanaged extraction source, not a governed document.
The `knowledge/PRD.md` induction is reverted (the file is removed).
`docs/planning/PRD.md` remains as ungoverned working material, its durable content
represented in the governed constitution, context, and ADRs, and it carries no lifecycle
`status` (its `status` field was stripped).

Authoring a governed PRD or feature spec later remains available as a separate,
forward-looking, human-initiated act (RKA ADR-0008), distinct from recovering existing
knowledge.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Keep the PRD inducted as governed `knowledge/PRD.md` (`type: prd`).**
   Rejected: the PRD's durable content already lives in the governed
   constitution/context/ADRs, so a governed PRD duplicates it and creates a drift-prone
   second source.
   A governed top-level PRD is the right home when the PRD is authored first as the
   product's requirements (greenfield); it is not the right fit for a brownfield repo
   whose knowledge was already extracted.
2. **Leave the halfway state (an ungoverned PRD carrying `status: draft`).**
   Rejected earlier and not revisited: RFC-001 section 2 forbids a `status` field on a
   document outside the governed root.

## Consequences

- One authoritative record of durable knowledge (the governed constitution, context, and
  ADRs); the PRD is working material that fed them, not a competing governed copy.
- The frontmatter validator governs the constitution, context, working-state pair, and
  ADRs; `docs/planning/PRD.md` is outside `knowledge/` and is not validated.
- Should the project ever need a governed, lifecycle-managed PRD or feature spec, it is
  authored deliberately at that point, not inherited by induction.
